Policy briefs and reports
======

Non-technical versions of research papers written for policymakers and practitioners. Each entry links back to the underlying academic work.

* [*Monetary policy under economic uncertainty: Evidence from the euro area*](https://www.suerf.org/publications/suerf-policy-notes-and-briefs/monetary-policy-under-economic-uncertainty-evidence-from-the-euro-area/), with M. Pfarrhofer and A. Stelzer. SUERF Policy Brief No. 1248, 2025. Based on [*On the effectiveness of the ECB's conventional and unconventional policies under uncertainty*]({{ base_path }}/publications/2021-06-01-hps-jebo/), *JEBO*, 2021.
* [*ECB's monetary policy in light of the Fed's policy stance*](https://www.suerf.org/publications/suerf-policy-notes-and-briefs/ecbs-monetary-policy-in-light-of-the-feds-policy-stance/), with F. Huber and T. O. Zörner. SUERF Policy Brief No. 838, 2024. Based on [*Hawks vs. Doves*]({{ base_path }}/institutional/2023-01-01-OeNB/), OeNB Working Paper 252, 2023.
* [*The impact of macroprudential policies on capital flows in CESEE*](https://www.suerf.org/publications/suerf-policy-notes-and-briefs/the-impact-of-macroprudential-policies-on-capital-flows-in-cesee/), with M. Eller, F. Huber, H. Schuberth, and L. Vashold. SUERF Policy Brief No. 199, 2021. Based on the [article of the same title]({{ base_path }}/publications/2021-02-01-ehhsv-jimf/), *JIMF*, 2021.
* [*Interest rates in Switzerland 1852–2020*]({{ base_path }}/institutional/2021-01-01-SECO/), with F. Huber, D. Kaufmann, R. Stuart, and C. Tille. Policy report for the State Secretariat for Economic Affairs (SECO), *Grundlagen für die Wirtschaftspolitik* No. 24, 2021. Based on [*What drives long-term interest rates?*]({{ base_path }}/wps/wp-01/) and the [SECO project]({{ base_path }}/grants/).
* *Wie sich ein zweiter Lockdown auf Österreichs Wirtschaft auswirken würde* (in German), with M. Pfarrhofer. *Die Presse*, blog of the Austrian Economic Association (NOeG), 2020.
